Overview

Creating an identity that feels simple, modern & memorable.

Asglow needed a visual identity that could work comfortably across different environments without losing recognition. The goal was to create a bold logo system that remained clear whether it appeared on a phone screen, business card, product or piece of clothing.

Rather than relying on unnecessary decoration, the identity focuses on a distinctive wordmark, strong contrast and a flexible visual language that allows the brand to remain consistent across multiple applications.

The Challenge

One identity.
Many applications.

The identity needed enough personality to be recognisable while remaining flexible enough to work across both small digital interfaces and larger physical applications. Scalability and clarity therefore became central to the direction of the logo.
